# Ringlite Support Knowledge Base

## Overview

- Ringlite is a business phone service. It gives a business its own phone numbers, calling from the browser, business texting, and one shared inbox where the whole team sees every call and text with a customer.
- The product covers: business numbers (local, toll-free, bring your own), calling (browser calls, menus, queues, voicemail), business texting (SMS, MMS, 10DLC done inside), a shared inbox for calls and texts, AI agents (answer calls, draft replies), a power dialer, fax, and integrations/API (webhooks, API keys).
- Ringlite is self-serve and set up in under an hour for most customers.
- Pricing is packaged: each plan is one monthly price that includes a set number of users and phone numbers. More users and numbers are add-ons.
- Team and Business include a fixed pool of call minutes the workspace shares. Starter is pay as you go. Calls past the pool and all texts use published rates.
- Ringlite does not sell "unlimited" calling. Call minutes and rates are published exactly.
- Every Ringlite account belongs to a verified person (identity verification) before it can call.
- Every number carries E911.
- Calls and texts reach the 48 contiguous US states only.
- Proof points Ringlite states about itself: every account identity-verified; carrier-registered texting; E911 on every number; rates published to the minute.

- A number carries up to 2 calls at once and about 150 outbound calls a day.
- Teams with heavy outbound calling give each caller their own number.
- Ringlite cannot promise how every carrier labels calls.
- Predictive dialers, robocalls, call centers and reselling are not allowed.

### 911 and E911

- Every number has E911.
- Calls to 911 from a Ringlite number go to the emergency centre for the address registered on that number.
- Keep the emergency address current in Settings, because 911 calls use it to send help.
- Change it in account menu → Edit profile → Emergency address (911).
- Each number has its own emergency address. Change it the same day you move desk, office or number. A wrong address can send help to the wrong place.
- Limits of internet calling: there is no service during a power or internet outage; the browser or device must be running and signed in; calling from somewhere other than the registered address may reach the wrong emergency centre; a call-back may fail if your number is not connected.
- Make sure everyone in the workspace knows these limits and that each of them has another way to call 911 (a mobile phone or a landline).
- The 911 disclosure page was last updated September 2026.

## Texting and 10DLC

- Business texting is available from the business number.
- For local US numbers, you register your brand and a 10DLC campaign inside Ringlite. Texting switches on after carriers approve it.
- 10DLC is the carrier registration every business needs to text from a local number in the US. Without it, carriers filter or block messages. Ringlite has the forms built in.
- Registration is done in Settings → Messaging.
- Until approved, texting is blocked.
- Individuals (sole proprietors) cannot text unregistered on any carrier.
- Carrier approval usually takes a few days to two weeks. Status is shown in the account, and Ringlite tells you when texting is live.
- Texting registration cost: a one-time registration charge plus a small monthly carrier fee, shown as one total at checkout before you pay.
- SMS text: 1.5¢ a segment.
- Picture messages (MMS): 3.5¢ each.
- Text bundle: 1,000 texts for $13.
- Contacts who have agreed to hear from you can be texted. Unsolicited bulk texting is not allowed and carriers block it.
- US law (the TCPA) and carrier rules require consent.
- Ringlite handles opt-out keywords like STOP automatically.
- Texting from a toll-free number uses a separate toll-free verification instead of 10DLC.
- Registration is built into the app. Fill in your brand and campaign once; Ringlite submits it and shows the status.
